## AnyPlay Launcher 0.3.4

A new first start, three more languages, and clips on the Mac.

### A new first start

The first start is done again from the ground up: calm, centred pages and only what a new player needs.

- **Language** first, with your system language already chosen.
- **Choose Your Look**: the three themes, and right there **Larger Text**, **High Contrast** and **Reduce Motion**.
- **Your Controller**, when a pad is connected: try the face buttons, pick which button confirms, and choose whether the Home button opens the launcher.
- **Where are your games?** shows what was found, so a wrong folder is noticed at once.
- **Play Together** says the privacy defaults in plain words before you sign in: private by default, Appear Offline, and your game files stay on your PC.
- **You're All Set** shows the Home button, where Settings are and how new games arrive.

Pad, keyboard and mouse work on every page.

### Español, Français, Português

- The launcher now speaks **Spanish**, **French** and **Brazilian Portuguese**, next to English, Turkish and German. Choose one in **Settings > Language** or on the first page.
- Games that follow the launcher's language get it too: Spanish (Spain or Latin America, from your system), French and Portuguese.
- Dates, numbers and sizes are written the way each language writes them.
- These translations have not been read by a native speaker yet. If you speak one of them, **Settings > Language > Improve a Translation** shows how to help.

### Mac

- **Clips on the Mac**: the Create button saves the last seconds of your game. Clips need Screen Recording for AnyPlay; when it is off, the launcher says so and opens the right page in System Settings. A clip key for the Mac comes later.

### Easier to use

- **Settings, Library and Notifications have a Back button** you can click, like Captures. Before, only Esc, the pad or a mouse side button left them.
- **Long names show in full** when you point at them or move the focus there: game tiles, notifications, overview rows and shelf captions.
- Screen readers now name the window buttons and the clip player.
- Smaller fixes: the Settings list no longer cuts off its last entry, the character count in Post a Listing no longer covers the hint, and the button bar shows the right buttons on more screens.

### Fixes

- **Share screen with game sound no longer crashes** on Windows. In 0.3.3 the sharing helper could stop the first time a stream with sound started.
- The online service is harder to misuse: tighter limits on what other players and servers can make it hold in memory, and game traffic reaches your game only from players in your session.

### Downloads
