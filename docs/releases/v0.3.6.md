## AnyPlay Launcher 0.3.6

Console Mode, a more colourful Daylight, and online screens that say what is going on.

### Console Mode

- **Settings > General > Console Mode** turns your PC into a console home: AnyPlay opens when you sign in to your computer, always fills the screen and starts ready for the controller.
- **Return to Desktop** sits right under it while Console Mode is on and closes the launcher, so your desktop is there as before.
- **Start with Windows** (on Linux and macOS: **Start at Login**) opens AnyPlay when you sign in, without the rest of Console Mode. It is off until you turn it on.
- **Open in Full Screen** now has its own row and applies right away.

### Daylight, with more colour

- Daylight is now a coloured morning sky: deep blue at the top, lilac across the middle and apricot at the horizon, with a golden sun, rose clouds and coloured lights drifting up.
- Panels let more of the sky through, so the look is less pale.
- Trophy grades and the focus ring are easier to see on Daylight.

### Online screens

- A friend request from someone you haven't played with shows **Accept** again.
- **Sign In** and **Create Account** open the Cloud page when no cloud is set, instead of doing nothing.
- Errors without their own message are shown in your language, and the details go to the log.
- Friends and Played With Recently say when you are offline or still connecting, with **Try Again**, instead of "No friends yet" or loading forever.
- Screen readers announce new notifications as new and name each notification's buttons.
- The party's voice mark and the "more below" fade are readable on Daylight.

### Online play with the AnyPlay Engine

- The online service and the game now prove their connection to each other with a challenge, so the game's sign-in never travels in the clear.
- If you use the **AnyPlay Engine** channel, update the engine too when the launcher offers it. Older engine builds can't connect to this version.

### Downloads

- Windows: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.6-Setup.exe` (installer, no administrator rights needed) or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.6-x64.exe` (one file).
- Linux: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.6-x86_64.AppImage`, `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.6-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.6-x86_64.flatpak`.
- macOS (Apple silicon, macOS 11 or newer): `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.6-macos-arm64.zip`. If macOS says the developer cannot be verified, open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.
- The launcher you have offers this update by itself, on Windows, Linux and the Mac. It is signed like every update since 0.2.0.
