## AnyPlay Launcher 0.3.3

The launcher starts in a blink, broadcasts carry your game's sound, and your profile, colours and controller light bar are yours to set.

### Faster

- **Starts in about a third of a second** instead of three to four on PCs with two graphics cards. The launcher no longer wakes the sleeping graphics card just to list it.

### Broadcast and sharing

- **Broadcasts carry the game's sound** on Windows: only the game, never your microphone or other programs.
- **YouTube streams over a secure connection** (`rtmps://`); a custom server can use `rtmp://` or `rtmps://`. If a secure connection fails, the Broadcast page says why in plain words.
- **Share screen carries the game's sound** on Windows too.

### Friends and profile

- **Public Profile** in **Settings > Online**: your page on the web stays off until you turn it on here or on the website.
- Friends who play together now read like "Playing … together · 2 of 4 players · You can join".
- Library tiles say **Play together** for games you can play with friends.

### Make it yours

- **Game Colors** remembers each game's colours, so the theme is there from the first frame.
- **Volumes** in **Settings > Sound** are real sliders: drag with the mouse, step with the arrows or the pad, Page Up and Page Down for bigger steps.
- **Any light bar colour** for your DualSense: Hue and Brightness sliders in the Controller Center.

### Home

- While a game is being removed, its tile on Home shows the progress. Thanks to Lenny (@Lenny4250gh) for this one.

### Downloads

- Windows: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.3-Setup.exe` (installer, no administrator rights needed) or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.3-x64.exe` (one file).
- Linux: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.3-x86_64.AppImage`, `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.3-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.3-x86_64.flatpak`.
- macOS (Apple silicon, macOS 11 or newer): `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.3-macos-arm64.zip`. If macOS says the developer cannot be verified, open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.
- The launcher you have offers this update by itself, on Windows, Linux and the Mac. It is signed like every update since 0.2.0.
