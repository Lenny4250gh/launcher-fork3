## AnyPlay Launcher 0.3.1

Find players for your games, send your screenshots and clips to friends, start a party in one press, and a cleaner look.

### New

- **Find Players** in a game's options: see who looks for a group for that game, with filters for the mode and the language, the places that are free and the time left.
  - **Post a listing** for an hour: the mode, a language, how many players you look for (1 to 7) and a short note.
  - **Ask to Join:** the player who posted accepts or declines. Accepting sends you a party invitation.
  - Report a listing or block its poster right from the list.
- **Send to a Friend** in Captures: send a screenshot (up to 10 MB) or a clip (up to 100 MB and 60 seconds) to a friend. It shows in your conversation as a picture or a play card, with Open, Report and, for your own, Unsend. Only that friend can open it, and it is deleted after 30 days.

### Friends

- **Add a Friend** is one clear page: type a name, press **Send Request**, and the result shows right there. A sent request clears the field; if it can't be sent, the page says why and keeps the name.
- Pasting `@name` or a friend link into the field works, and a friend link you open while signed out waits until you sign in.
- The buttons on a friend's row no longer flicker when you point at them.

### Party

- **Start a Party** opens your party room right away; invite friends from inside it. A party of one says "Your party is open" with **Invite Friends** as the main button.
- **Voice Settings** sit next to Mute and Deafen in the party room, and no longer show when you are not in a party.

### Cleaner

- **Activity** is just recent activity now: the reaction buttons are gone, and an item opens the player's profile.
- Empty pages (Notifications, Captures, a game without trophies and more) are centred on the screen.
- The page list in **Settings** has the same gap between every entry.
- The shelves on Home (Recently Played and the others) show larger icons with even gaps, and fewer games when the window is narrow instead of squeezing them.
- The trophy count next to **Play** is as tall as the Play button.
