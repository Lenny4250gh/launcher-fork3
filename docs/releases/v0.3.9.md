## AnyPlay Launcher 0.3.9

Share play invitations you cannot miss, block lists that reach your games, and a safer way for updates to arrive.

### Online

- **Share play invitations now land in Notifications.** When a friend in your party invites you to share play, the invitation stays in the Notification Center with **Accept** and **Dismiss**, and the bell lights up, even when your library is empty. Before, it showed only as a toast and on the party page, so it was easy to miss before it ran out.
- **The Share Play row** on the Friends home page now shows an invitation even when you are not in a party yet.
- **Games that read your block list** now get the players you blocked on AnyPlay, instead of an error.

### Updates

- **A bad update can be stopped.** If a version turns out to be broken, we can now pull it before it reaches you, and a new version can go out to players in stages. Your launcher checks this in the signed update information it already reads; nothing about you is sent.

### Downloads

- **Windows:** `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.9-Setup.exe` (installer, no administrator rights needed) or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.9-x64.exe` (one file).
- **Linux:** `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.9-x86_64.AppImage`, `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.9-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.9-x86_64.flatpak`.
- **macOS** (Apple silicon, macOS 11 or newer): `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.9-macos-arm64.zip`. If macOS says the developer cannot be verified, open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.
- The launcher you have offers this update by itself, on Windows, Linux and the Mac. It is signed like every update since 0.2.0.
