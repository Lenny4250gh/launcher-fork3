## AnyPlay Launcher 0.3.7

Party voice without echo, the console flow on a controller from start to finish, and Clips on Linux.

### Party voice

- **No more echo on speakers.** Your microphone no longer sends your friends' voices back to them when you play without a headset.
- **Echo Cancellation** in the voice settings, under Reduce Background Noise, is on by default. If your own voice sounds cut off while others talk, for example with a headset, turn it off.

### A console from start to finish

- **Sign in and update with a controller.** Every step of signing in and updating works with a pad alone, and the hint bar always shows how to go back.
- While you sign in in the browser, the launcher says that the controller works again once you return, and it comes back to the front by itself when the sign-in ends.
- **Settings > Connected Apps** now shows, for your system, how to start AnyPlay as your console home: Console Mode, Steam Big Picture, and Steam's Gaming Mode on Linux.

### Clips on Linux

- **Clips now work on Linux.** The desktop asks for the game's window when a game starts, and the launcher says so.
- If no window is chosen, Clips stay off for that game and the launcher tells you why.
- Linux has no clip key yet. Press Create on your controller to save a clip.

### Unlocking games

- **Pause and resume an unlock**, with a separate **Cancel** that asks before it throws the progress away.
- Closing the launcher during an unlock asks first, and **Pause and Quit** picks it up again later. Thanks to Lenny (@Lenny4250gh).

### Online

- **Messages** say when they are loading or when you are offline, with **Try Again**, instead of showing an empty list.
- A conversation that fails to load can be loaded again.
- A profile that no longer exists says so.
- A sign-in that you stop in the browser now shows a notice.
- A voice connection that fails reads **Not working** instead of **Off**.
- Text that is cut short can be read in full under the pointer or the focus.
- **The account pages on the web open in your language** when you come from the launcher.

### Looks

- Trophy grade labels are easy to read on every look.
- The focus ring, profile and activity cards are clearer on **Daylight**.

### Mac

- **The online area on the Mac** now runs the online service inside the app, with party voice through your Mac's microphone and speakers.

### More

- **The phone controller** can't be turned off while a phone is paired. Thanks to fogydes (@fogydes).
- **On Linux**, sharing no longer locks up on PCs where two video encoders start at once. Shared game sound no longer clicks after lost packets. Thanks to fma-rc (@fma-rc).

### Flatpak

The Flatpak's app ID is now `com.anyplaynet.Launcher`, ready for Flathub. Bundles up to 0.3.5 used `net.anyplay.Launcher`. To keep your settings, remove the old one and move its folder before the first start of the new one:

```sh
flatpak uninstall --user net.anyplay.Launcher
mv ~/.var/app/net.anyplay.Launcher ~/.var/app/com.anyplaynet.Launcher
```

### Downloads

- **Windows:** `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.7-Setup.exe` (installer, no administrator rights needed) or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.7-x64.exe` (one file).
- **Linux:** `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.7-x86_64.AppImage`, `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.7-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.7-x86_64.flatpak`.
