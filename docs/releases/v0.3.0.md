## AnyPlay Launcher 0.3.0

The biggest update so far: talk in games, go live, see which games run well, set up your controllers, and use the launcher in German or on the Steam Deck.

### Play together

- **Voice in games:**
  - **Push to talk** now works while a game is in front. Choose a key, or a **Talk Button** on your controller (a bumper, trigger or stick click).
  - Soft sounds tell you when the microphone opens and closes.
  - **Voice Activity** is now called **Open Mic**.
  - **Reduce Background Noise** is on by default. Turn it off in the voice settings of the Online area.
- **Go live** (Windows): stream to Twitch, YouTube or your own RTMP server.
  - Set it up in **Settings > Broadcast**. Your stream key is kept in the Windows Credential Manager.
  - While a game runs, the Home button panel offers **Start Broadcast**, and a LIVE mark shows in the launcher.
  - Game sound is not in the stream yet.
- **Notification Center:** a bell with a count in the top bar opens one list of friend requests, invitations, messages, trophies and updates. Accept, Join or Open right from the list.
- **Friends' status:** the friends list shows who is online and what they play.
- **Invite links:** **Copy Invite Link** on the party page gives you a link to send to friends. A friend link opens **Add Friend** with the name already filled in; it sends nothing by itself.
- **Emoji:** a quiet smiley in the message field opens your system's emoji picker (Windows and macOS).

### Your library

- **How games run:** each game shows what players found: **Runs well**, **Runs with issues** or **Doesn't start**, and **LAN** for games that play together on a local network.
- **Sort, filter and search:**
  - Sort by name, last played, size or status.
  - Show only ready games, games that need unlocking, or games you can play together.
  - Search works with the keyboard and with a pad.
- **Storage:** see how much space each unlocked copy uses and how much is free, and remove an unlocked copy. Saves are backed up first, and your game files are never touched.
- **Game Hub:** one page per game with its trophies, play time, captures, friends playing, Play Together and how it runs. Open it from Home or with Options on a game in the library.
  - A trophy overview of all your games, newest first.
  - Your play time this week, in the hub and on your profile.
  - Trophy banners show the trophy's grade and, when known, how many players have it.
- **Home:** quiet shelves for Friends Playing Now (with Join), Recently Played and New. New games announce themselves, and the newest one is selected.

### Make it yours

- **Controller Center** in **Settings > Controllers**: see every connected controller with its battery, test each button and stick, test vibration, set the stick dead zone and the DualSense light bar colour.
  - Remap the launcher's buttons on pad and keyboard, or confirm with Circle.
  - A troubleshooter helps when a controller is not found or presses twice.
- **Settings** has a page for every feature: General, Appearance, Library, Sound, Controllers, Captures, Broadcast, Online, Connected Apps, Language, Toolkit, Advanced and About. The list remembers the page you left.
- **Game Colors:** a theme that takes its colours from the selected game.
- **Volumes:** set the volume for each kind of sound in **Settings > Sound**.
- **Larger Text** and **High Contrast** in **Settings > Appearance**.
- **Drag to scroll** lists with the mouse.
- **German** (Deutsch): the launcher now speaks German, with German dates and numbers. It picks German by itself when your system is set to German, or choose it in **Settings > Language**.

### Everywhere you play

- **Steam Deck:** in Gaming Mode the launcher opens full screen and is ready for the pad, with pad hints.
- **On-screen keyboard:** every text field opens a keyboard when you select it with a pad.
- **Linked copies:** an unlocked copy can link to the game files on another drive instead of copying them, which saves space. On Windows this needs Developer Mode or administrator rights; otherwise the launcher copies as before.
- **Flatpak:** a Flatpak bundle for Linux is a new download.
- **Mac:** **Add to Steam** works on the Mac, and the **PS button** on your controller opens the launcher there too.

### Steadier

- When a game ends, its log says why: you quit, the window closed, it crashed, or the launcher stopped it.
- A game that writes the same line over and over no longer floods its log.
- When a game ends in trouble, **Send Report** on the notice opens the report for you to read before you send it. It now includes your controllers and the end of the online log.
- When the launcher stops responding, it notes where in its log, so we can find the cause.

### Cleaner

- Offline, friend rows no longer offer to join, and the party room hides what can't work. Connecting and offline look different now.
- The trophy list has a Back button and button hints.
- **What's New** shows once after an update. Open it again from **Settings > About**.

### Downloads

- Windows: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.0-Setup.exe` (installer, no administrator rights needed) or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.0-x64.exe` (one file).
- Linux: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.0-x86_64.AppImage`, `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.0-x86_64.flatpak`.
- macOS (Apple silicon, macOS 11 or newer): `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.0-macos-arm64.zip`. If macOS says the developer cannot be verified, open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.
- The launcher you have offers this update by itself, on Windows, Linux and the Mac. It is signed like every update since 0.2.0.
