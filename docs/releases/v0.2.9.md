## AnyPlay Launcher 0.2.9

A fix for 0.2.8.

### Fixes

- Opening a conversation in **Friends > Messages** no longer freezes the launcher. In 0.2.8 the launcher stopped responding as soon as a conversation opened, and Windows closed it.

### Downloads

- Windows: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.9-Setup.exe` (installer, no administrator rights needed) or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.9-x64.exe` (one file).
- Linux: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.9-x86_64.AppImage` or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.9-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`.
- macOS (Apple silicon, macOS 11 or newer): `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.9-macos-arm64.zip`.
- The launcher you have offers this update by itself, on Windows, Linux and, from 0.2.8 on, the Mac. It is signed like every update since 0.2.0.

News and help: the AnyPlay Discord, linked in the [README](https://github.com/anyplaynet/launcher#readme).
