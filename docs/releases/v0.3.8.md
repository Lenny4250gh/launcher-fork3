## AnyPlay Launcher 0.3.8

Who sees your leaderboard scores, and three more screens that work with a pad alone.

### Online

- **Leaderboard Scores** on the Account page: choose who sees your scores on game leaderboards, **Friends**, **Everyone** or **Only You**. Friends is the default, and a line under the row says what your choice means. It is the same choice as on the account web, in all six languages.
- **Video for Share Screen** now says what to do when the download fails or the file cannot be used, instead of showing a web address or a system error. The details are in the log.

### A console from start to finish

- **The Log, the report review and What's New work with a pad or the keyboard alone.** Left and Right move between their buttons, and the hint bar shows how to select and go back.

### Downloads

- **Windows:** `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.8-Setup.exe` (installer, no administrator rights needed) or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.8-x64.exe` (one file).
- **Linux:** `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.8-x86_64.AppImage`, `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.8-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.8-x86_64.flatpak`.
- **macOS** (Apple silicon, macOS 11 or newer): `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.8-macos-arm64.zip`. If macOS says the developer cannot be verified, open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.
- The launcher you have offers this update by itself, on Windows, Linux and the Mac. It is signed like every update since 0.2.0.
