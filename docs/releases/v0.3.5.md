## AnyPlay Launcher 0.3.5

Three new looks that move, a calmer language choice, and a safer clips folder.

### New looks

- **Ember**: a low, warm hearth of charcoal and copper in AnyPlay orange. Embers rise slowly and fade, warm smoke drifts over them.
- **Tide**: deep, cool water in petrol and ice blue, with threads of light from the surface and slow light shafts.
- **Daylight**: the first light look. Soft daylight, dark text, a cool sky over a warm horizon, clouds drifting by.
- Choose them in **Settings > Appearance** or on the Look page of the first start, which now shows all six looks in two rows.
- With **Animations: Reduced**, every look holds still.

### Played With Recently

- **Friends > Played With Recently** lists the people you shared a party or a game session with in the last 30 days, so you can add them as a friend or look at their profile in one step.
- Only you see this list. Turn off **Remember People I Play With** or clear the list any time, here or in your privacy settings on the web. People you block don't show up.

### Easier to read

- **Choosing a language** is now a plain list of names, on the first start and in **Settings > Language**. The language you point at shows the page in it right away.
- **Loading** with **Animations: Reduced** no longer spins or sweeps: the indicator pulses gently instead.

### Clips

- Clips are saved only into your Captures folder. If something tries to save a clip elsewhere, the launcher says so.

### Under the hood

- The online service holds up better against misbehaving connections: limits on what a game or another player can make it wait for or keep, and connections that go silent are closed. Thanks to fogydes (@fogydes) for several of these.
- On Linux, hardware video encoding for sharing stays off for now while it is tested on more graphics cards. Sharing keeps working as before.

### Downloads

- Windows: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.5-Setup.exe` (installer, no administrator rights needed) or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.5-x64.exe` (one file).
- Linux: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.5-x86_64.AppImage`, `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.5-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.5-x86_64.flatpak`.
- macOS (Apple silicon, macOS 11 or newer): `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.3.5-macos-arm64.zip`. If macOS says the developer cannot be verified, open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.
- The launcher you have offers this update by itself, on Windows, Linux and the Mac. It is signed like every update since 0.2.0.
